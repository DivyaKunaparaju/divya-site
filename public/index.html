About

I turn ambiguous mandates into structured, cross-functional execution.

For the past 15+ years I've been the operator MNCs and scale-ups bring in when there's a big mandate and no clear playbook — a market to enter, a transformation to land, a capability gap to close. I build the system it runs on, align the stakeholders it depends on, and stay close enough to execution that the mandate doesn't get lost on the way down.

That pattern has held across very different rooms: early technology delivery inside two of Singapore's largest insurers, market-entry and venture strategy for industrial and logistics clients across APAC, manufacturing transformation work that shaved real cost out of a plant floor. It scaled from there — a $40M digital venture built from zero across APAC with Boston Consulting Group, the OKR and governance backbone for a nine-market scale-up at Circles.Life, and now enterprise-wide capability transformation at Singtel spanning thousands of roles across AI, 5G, and Cyber. The throughline is the same at every size: operating models and governance systems built well enough that someone else can run them, and enough comfort translating a CXO's intent into something a delivery team can execute against.

To build conviction on where transformation is heading, I went hands-on. Through the SIT x NVIDIA AI Centre (SNAIC) programme, I work directly with LLMs, RAG pipelines, and Agentic AI systems. It's not a pivot into engineering — my first degree was in computer science, which is part of why this comes naturally — it's how I pressure-test feasibility, challenge assumptions, and work credibly with the technical teams delivering AI-driven change.

That combination — programme discipline and direct technical fluency — is what lets me hold both the strategy and the delivery risk in the same room.
